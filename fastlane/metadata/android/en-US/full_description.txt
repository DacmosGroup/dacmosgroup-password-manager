Dacmos Password Manager keeps your passwords encrypted on your own device, with no DacmosGroup accounts or servers.

ZERO-KNOWLEDGE
Your master password never leaves the device. Neither DacmosGroup nor anyone else can read your vault: if you forget the master password, the data cannot be recovered.

ENCRYPTION
- AES-256-GCM
- PBKDF2-SHA256 with 600,000 iterations (OWASP 2024)
- Native Web Crypto API, no third-party crypto libraries

FEATURES
- Fingerprint unlock (BiometricPrompt + Android Keystore)
- TOTP (2FA) code generator with countdown
- Password health: weak, reused and breached (k-anonymity lookup: only a hash prefix leaves the device)
- Import CSV from Google Password Manager, Bitwarden, LastPass and 1Password
- Export an encrypted backup and CSV with Android's "Save as" picker (Downloads, Drive or any location you choose)
- Configurable auto-lock
- Spanish, English and Portuguese (BR) interface

YOUR BACKUP IS UP TO YOU
Your passwords live only on your phone and nobody else can read them, not even us. That is why, if you lose your phone, only you can recover them: keep an encrypted backup (Settings → Export backup) in Drive or on your computer.
- When you switch phones, export your encrypted backup and import it on the new one; the app does not copy your data through Android's automatic backups.
- To bring an existing vault to Android, export the encrypted backup from the PWA or the extension and import it in the app.

Notes:
- Biometric unlock requires a registered fingerprint (face recognition on some phones does not meet the required security level).
- Google Drive/OneDrive sync: available in the extension and the PWA; coming to Android in a future version.
