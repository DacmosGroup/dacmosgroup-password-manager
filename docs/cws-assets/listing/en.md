## Description

🔒 Dacmos Password Manager — Enterprise-grade security for everyone

Manage your passwords with the same encryption standard used by banks and governments — no external servers involved.

🌐 ZERO-KNOWLEDGE MODEL
Your master password never leaves your device. Neither DacmosGroup nor anyone else can access your data. Only you.

🔐 BANK-GRADE ENCRYPTION
- AES-256-GCM (NIST FIPS 197)
- PBKDF2-SHA256 with 600,000 iterations (OWASP 2024)
- Native Web Crypto API — no third-party libraries

🌎 NEW IN v0.5.0 — YOUR LANGUAGE
Interface available in English, Español and Português (BR).
It automatically matches your Chrome language — zero setup.

⚡ KEY FEATURES v0.5.0

Encrypted vault: Logins, Credit cards and Identities
Import from Google Password Manager, Bitwarden, LastPass, 1Password
Built-in TOTP generator (2FA) with countdown — free
Password Health: detects weak, reused and breached passwords
Export to generic CSV and Bitwarden-compatible CSV
Autofill on login, checkout and sign-up forms
Robust URL matching: subdomains, wildcards and base domain
☁️ BYOC sync: Google Drive and OneDrive — your data, your cloud
☁️ Zero-Knowledge in transit: your cloud provider never sees your data
📱 Multi-device: restore your vault on any new device
🔑 Forgot your password? Create a fresh vault right from the lock screen
